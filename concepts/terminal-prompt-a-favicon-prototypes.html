Three Terminal Prompt A marks

Each mark keeps the letter A legible at favicon size while carrying a small terminal cue. The preview row is the practical test: the 16px version matters more than the large showcase.

01 / Prompt Angle

The clearest terminal read

A monoline A carries a rose prompt chevron. It is the most literal translation of “Terminal Prompt A,” with the strongest recognition in a tab.

Best for: a practical operator identity with a little warmth.
